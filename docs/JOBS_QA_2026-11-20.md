# Jobs QA, 2026-11-20: whole jobs across tabs

Earlier rounds audited one page at a time (docs/USABILITY_AUDIT_2026-11-14.md, docs/SPEC_PARITY_AUDIT_2026-11-16.md,
docs/UI_REDESIGN_2026-11-16.md). This round follows eight jobs the way a fabricator does them, across tabs, in headless
Chromium at 1440 px and 400 px, typing values and using the page's own presets and buttons. For each job:
NaN / undefined / Infinity / null in the tab text, sideways page scroll, console errors, screenshots looked at (JPEG,
outside the repository, deleted), a lone-unit scan (the checker of easyflow_ui.js run over the result rows), cross-tab
consistency of the quantities the job carries, and hand checks of at least two results against the formula the page
cites.

Hard rules kept: no calculation's result, default, input id, project-file key, URL hash or bridge contract changed; no
pinned test number changed; no external resources. Every fix has a regression check in `web/tests/jobs_ui.js`
(checked: 41 of its checks fail on the page before the fixes, git 9a3d05b; all 59 pass after).

## Job 2: 4-into-1 header, K24A2 at 8,000 RPM

Done: Engine on its own → K24A2; Headers tab, Tuning RPM 8000, "All cylinders into one", "Use 1.625 in tube";
4-Stroke tab; Quick tab at 8,000 RPM, Top end.

| Quantity | Formula and source | Hand | Page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runner length | L = 850 × (180 + 60) ÷ 8,000 − 3 in (page rule) | 22.50 in | 22.5 in (572 mm) |
| Primary ID | RPM ÷ 88,200 × cu in per cylinder at peak torque 4,500; 143.6 ÷ 4 = 35.9 | A = 1.832 in², d = 1.527 in | 1.53 in (38.8 mm), 1.83 in² |
| Merge cone, 15° included | (Dc − Dp) ÷ 2 ÷ tan 7.5° | (67.9 − 38.0) ÷ 2 ÷ 0.1317 = 113.6 mm | 4.5 in (114 mm) |
| Collector rule of thumb | 1.75 to 2.00 × primary OD (1.625 in) | 2.84 to 3.25 in | 2.84 in (72.2 mm) to 3.25 in (82.5 mm) |

No defect to fix. Proposals and notes:
- P4 The Headers tab's "Smith's 1950s open-pipe rule" passes bore 0, stroke 0 and 2 valves (index.html, the
  `b5LengthSection` call in the Headers render), so for a K24 it uses an estimated 96.5 × 80 mm bore and stroke and a
  2-valve head and gives 44.1 in; the 4-Stroke tab's same row uses the 87 × 99 mm, 4 valves of the preset and gives
  37.6 in. Proposal (it changes a displayed comparison figure): read f-bore, f-stroke and f-valves when the Headers
  engine is the 4-Stroke tab's.
- Tuning RPM is an input of each tab: 8,000 typed on Headers leaves the 4-Stroke tab at the preset's 6,800, so the same
  engine shows 22.5 in (Headers) and 27.0 in (4-Stroke) primaries. By design (separate calculators; Quick's "Fill in
  every tab" sets both), but a fabricator moving between them is not told. Proposal: a one-line note on each when
  they differ.
- Quick tab: typing the peak power RPM clears the preset's peak-torque RPM, so the primary is sized at 0.7 × 8,000
  (1.70 in, a 1.875 in tube) against the Headers tab's 1.53 in at 4,500 RPM. The page says so ("Peak torque RPM is
  taken as 70% …"), left as is.
