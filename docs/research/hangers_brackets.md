## 3. Laser cutting and bending rules

**SendCutSend, general rules.** Hole to edge ≥ 1 × hole diameter. Slots ≥ 1.5 × slot width from edges. Hole diameter and bridge ≥ 50 % of thickness as the absolute minimum; 1–1.5 × thickness is preferred for strength. Typical tolerance ±0.005 in ([blog](https://sendcutsend.com/blog/basic-tolerances-and-cut-feature-relationships/), [sheet-cutting](https://sendcutsend.com/guidelines/sheet-cutting/)).

Material-page rules:
- **5052:** hole ≥ t; hole to edge ≥ 2t; bend radius "as low as 1t" ([5052](https://sendcutsend.com/materials/5052-aluminum/)).
- **6061:** hole ≥ t; edge distance 1.5–2t ([6061](https://sendcutsend.com/materials/6061-aluminum/)).
- **304:** webs ≥ t; inside bend radius ≥ t, or 2–3t for 0.250 in and thicker; flange ≥ 3t ([304](https://sendcutsend.com/materials/stainless-steel/)).
- **Mild steel:** inside bend radius ≥ 1t ([MS](https://sendcutsend.com/materials/mild-steel/)).

## 6. Hanger loads, spacing and stiffness
- SAE 2014-01-1708: places hangers using ADDOFD from a free modal analysis and checks hanger force under unit sinusoidal powertrain excitation. The abstract gives no numbers ([SAE](https://papers.sae.org/publications/technical-papers/content/2014-01-1708/)).
- MEIC-14 paper: applies a 100,000 N·mm sinusoidal torque at the powertrain centre of gravity and reports hanger reaction forces below 5 N. Constrained modes must avoid the engine idle frequency ([Atlantis](https://atlantis-press.com/proceedings/meic-14/15264)).
- Deeds: mount dog-bone hangers in line with the exhaust so the body can swing as the system grows with heat ([Deeds](https://deedsengineering.com/products.json)).
- Dynamic load factor (g), maximum span between hangers and isolator stiffness values: **n/f**.
