# Fabrication audit of the cut, bend, print and template outputs, 2026-11-11

Scope: everything the owner measures, cuts, prints or bends from: cope (fishmouth) templates, bend tables and cut
lists, X-pipe build steps, merge collector plates and flat patterns, cone blanks, muffler chamber cut files, hanger and
bracket DXFs, flange locator presets, tack-jig cradles and saddles, and the units on all of them. Not audited: the
print-split lap joints (audited before), the CFD tube export and STL code in cadtools.py, and the embedded intake CFD
block (owned elsewhere).

Method (the physics audit's discipline). Each output was found by grepping the page and the add-in, read, then
recomputed from geometric first principles in code written for this audit (not the page's formula): bisection on the
pipe surfaces, marching lines into a cylinder, sampling solids on a fine grid, rebuilding a 3-D centreline from the
bend table alone, and summing developed arc lengths. The independent references are asserted in
`fusion-addin/tests/test_fabaudit.py` and `web/tests/fabaudit_ui.js`. "Numeric" below means recomputed on the cases
listed; "read" means only read and reasoned about. Tolerances: 0.05 mm on template points, 0.25 mm on lengths, 0.1 deg
on angles (0.025 mm and 0.01 deg in the add-in tests). Lengths are in mm with inches where a case is given in inches.

Line numbers are `web/index.html` and the add-in files after this audit's edits.

## Questionable items for the conductor (no edit made)

- Q6. Two fishmouth depths for one tee: the page's paper template (inside-edge line, 6.35 mm rise for 1.5 in ID on
  2.5 in) and the Fusion tee note (outside edge, 7.68 mm). Both are right for their method (square-to-wall paper cut vs
  a true cope); the tee note could say "for a true cope (hole saw or the model); a square paper cut is shallower".
- Q7. `inMm` rounds inches to 1/16 (up to 0.79 mm off) while the mm figure beside it is to 0.5 mm (0.25 mm). The brief
  asks for 1/64 in; the owner's tape is likely 1/16. Over a cut list the errors do not accumulate in the mm column.
- Q8. The cope template has no direction or flank marks and no separate scale bar; the strip width (pi d) is the scale
  check. A paper print path (PDF/SVG with a 100 mm bar) does not exist; only DXF.
- Q9. Cone blanks (page `coneFlat`, add-in `merge.cone_flat`) put the mid-wall at ID/2 + w/2 radially; the true offset
  normal to the wall is w/2 / cos(half angle). For 15 deg included that is 0.007 mm on 1.651 mm wall: negligible, but
  the add-in's spike uses the exact form, so the two are not the same rule.
- Q10. The 2-Stroke tab itself shows cone lengths with the coarse `clen` form (0.1 in / 1 mm); not changed (calculator
  page, not a cut sheet), but the owner will cut from it.
- Q11. Hanger "along" fins map the page's 2-D part into the model mirrored (x to Z, y to Y, normal X, so x cross y = -X).
  Harmless for a flat plate cut from the DXF (it can be turned over), but an asymmetric fin's holes land mirrored
  along the tube from the page's picture. Needs a Fusion check before calling it a defect.

## Inventory, method, cases and results

Category counts are the outputs checked numerically / read only.

### 1. Cope (fishmouth) templates: 2 numeric

- Page `copeTemplate(d, D, extra, w)` 14533, DXF from `button[data-cope]` (Mufflers tab: neck 14779, tee 14795).
  Reference: main surface height at lateral offset y by bisection on y^2 + z^2 = R^2; a square-to-wall cut must stop at
  the highest main surface under any point of the wall (radii ri to ro, 40 steps). Cases: 1.5 in ID neck (41.402 mm
  OD) on 2.5 in (63.5 mm), 16 ga; same size 50.8 on 50.8 mm; small d/D thick wall, 25.4 on 76.2 mm, 3.05 mm (0.120 in).
  Results: every one of 72 points within 0.000 mm; strip length = pi d (130.07, 159.59, 79.80 mm); outline closed;
  `$INSUNITS 4`, `$MEASUREMENT 1`, `%.4f` mm, `AC1009`. No wall: the outside-edge line as before (callers with no wall).
  Seam: x = 0 and pi d are in line with the main pipe (the branch's shortest point, z = 0); straight edge at H on the
  square-cut end; what the paper covers is cut away (checked: removal H at the in-line points, H - rise at the flanks).
- Add-in `templates.tee_cope` 359 (depth used by the Fusion solid and the tee note). Reference: each of 360 lines of the
  branch's outside marched into the cylinder then bisected. Cases: square 1.5 on 2.5 in (6.35 mm = R - sqrt(R^2 - r^2)),
  same size 2.5 on 2.5 in (31.75 mm), a 1.25 in branch offset 0.5 in (12.7 mm) from the main axis (15.26 mm), a 45 deg
  lateral 1.5 on 2.5 in (19.05 mm). All within 0.025 mm. OK.
